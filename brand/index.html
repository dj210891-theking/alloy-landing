01

Who He Is

The brand is a Magician — he transforms base metal into meaning, the ordinary into the significant. He walks into the room and says almost nothing, yet the room slowly reorients around him. He is cold on the surface, mysterious by discipline — he never explains himself fully, and this is precisely why people lean in. And beneath the stillness he is an instigator: every object he hands you is a dare. Wear it and start something.

03

Color

Pure monochrome. No accent. Ever. Three non-colors, disciplined absolutely — because color is noise, and the brand is a whisper. When people see uninterrupted black, bone, and chrome, they should know whose room they're standing in.

Void
#0A0B0D
The ground. 80% of every surface — packaging, site, film. Matte, light-absorbing.
Bone
#E9E6DF
The voice. All type and marks. Never pure white — bone is aged, ancient-future.
Chrome
#C9C9CE → gradient
The gleam. Reserved for the objects themselves and one highlight per composition.
RULE — IF A DESIGN FEELS LIKE IT NEEDS COLOR, IT NEEDS MORE EMPTINESS INSTEAD.
04

The Mark

Two masses — one solid, one hollow — held apart forever. They never touch; the gap between them is the actual logo. Solid is matter, hollow is meaning. On jewellery it is stamped as a hallmark; in film it is two bodies that never quite meet.

Letters With Gaps

06

What He Feels Like

Three materials, one hierarchy. Matte black is the ground — boxes, bags, backdrops, light-absorbing. Mirror chrome is the single gleam — the object itself, the only thing allowed to shine. And where surfaces meet, raw collides with refined: rough cast textures against machined edges, the transformation from ore to object left deliberately visible. The magic trick, mid-reveal.

Ground
Matte void — absorbs light, 80% of everything
Gleam
Mirror chrome — one polished element per composition
Story
Raw cast meets machined edge — transformation left visible
